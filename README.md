# SwordVow

A vow-keeping, habit-forging PWA. Not another habit tracker — a covenant with yourself.

Make a vow. Seal your daily progress. When your blade breaks, reforge it with kintsugi. Scars stay. Your journey lives on.

## What makes it different
- **A vow, not a checkbox** — Build a solemn commitment to yourself, not a to-do list.
- **Break & reforge** — Miss a day? Your blade breaks and the break is kept in the record. Reflect, then reforge with gold. No erasing, no fake restarts.
- **The record never lies** — Every seal and every scar is preserved. Your long-term self-commitment timeline.
- **Digital blade cards** — Share your journey as a card, with your privacy fully in your control.
